Our Story

A family kitchen, out on the High Road

Pitta Corner is a family-run Greek & Cypriot grill on Wood Green's High Road, serving the neighbourhood breakfast through to late-night dinner. Everything is cooked fresh to order on the open grill you can see from the counter — gyros carved off the spit, skewers turned by hand, pittas warmed and stuffed the moment you order.

It's fast food the honest way: real ingredients, generous portions, and the same recipes that have fed Greek and Cypriot families for generations — no shortcuts, just good grilling.
