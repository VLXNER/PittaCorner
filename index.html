Our Story

A family grill on a
busy High Road

Pitta Corner sits at 43 High Road, a few minutes from Wood Green tube — a family-run Greek and Cypriot kitchen wedged into a parade of shops, with a dark blue front, a turquoise sign, and window boxes spilling over with petunias and ivy. People spot the flowers first and the charcoal second.

It works three ways at once: a coffee stop in the morning, a souvlaki counter at lunch, a proper sit-down grill at night. The grill runs the whole time, in view of the counter, so the wrap in your hand was on the coals about ninety seconds ago.

Skewers seasoned with lemon and oregano. Gyros carved off the spit. Fries cut from whole potatoes. Tzatziki and htipiti mixed in-house. Nothing clever — just the things worth doing properly.
