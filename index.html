Our Story

A family kitchen
at number 43

Pitta Corner sits a few minutes from Wood Green tube — a family-run Greek and Cypriot kitchen in a parade of shops, with a dark blue front and window boxes spilling over with petunias. People spot the flowers first and the charcoal second.

The grill runs all day, in view of the counter. The wrap in your hand was over the coals about ninety seconds ago.

It works three ways at once: a coffee and breakfast stop in the morning, a souvlaki counter at lunch, and a proper sit-down grill at night. Skewers seasoned with lemon and oregano, gyros off the spit, fries cut from whole potatoes, tzatziki and spicy feta mixed in-house.

On The Grill

What we won't
cut corners on

Four things the kitchen does the long way round, every day, because the short way tastes like the short way.

Charcoal souvla

Lamb, kokoretsi and baby chicken turned on the spit, alongside skewers of pork, chicken, beef and village loukaniko.

Hand-cut fries

Cut from whole potatoes each day, and they go inside the wrap rather than beside it — the Greek way.

Homemade dips

Tzatziki, houmous and spicy feta trokafteri, all mixed in our own kitchen.

Boards to share

The Mix Grill Platter for two or four — pittas, fries, dips, skewers, gyros and lamb chops on one board.

The Charcoal

Everything starts
over the coals

Skewers along the bars, the spit turning behind them, from eight in the morning until close. It is the first thing you smell on the High Road.

How It's Made

Five steps
and about ten minutes

Nothing here is finished before you order it. This is the run a wrap makes from the fridge to your hand.

01

Onto the skewer

Pork, chicken, beef and lamb threaded by hand onto kalamakia, with village loukaniko alongside them on the rail.

02

Over the coals

Real charcoal, in full view of the counter, running from eight in the morning until close. Lemon and oregano go on at the grill.

03

Carved off the spit

Gyros is shaved to order as the spit turns, never cut in advance and never held under a lamp.

04

Chips cut that day

Whole potatoes cut in the shop each morning, fried fresh, and they go inside the wrap rather than in a bag beside it.

05

Wrapped and handed over

Warm pitta, tzatziki or the special sauce, onion and tomato, folded tight in paper. About ninety seconds off the coals.

Good To Know

Questions we get
at the counter

The short answers. For anything about allergens, ingredients or today's prices, the staff at the counter are the authority.

What are your opening hours?

Every day from 8am to 11pm — breakfast and coffee from eight, and the grill runs all day until close.

Do you deliver?

Yes — Just Eat, Deliveroo and Uber Eats all carry the menu across N22 and beyond. Or call 020 8826 9594 and collect it hot from the counter.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan dishes?

Yes — look for the tags on the board: halloumi kalamaki as a wrap, portion or skepasti (vegetarian), and the falafel wrap and portion (vegan). Several salads, dips and sides are meat-free too — ask a member of staff about ingredients and allergens.

What's the £250 Lamb Souvla?

A whole lamb, slow-turned on the spit. It's pre-order only, so call ahead and give us notice.

Why do some prices say “ask in store”?

Those prices were recently changed by hand on our board, and we'd rather tell you the current figure at the counter than publish one that's out of date.

Do you do halal?

The breakfast menu has halal versions — beef sausage and turkey rashers in place of pork. For any other dish, ask a member of staff before ordering.
